See it

Most of the screens in your life are built to keep you checking, scrolling and coming back. Slipstream is built to give that time back. It holds your notifications until you pause, shows you what's going on without asking for a look, and measures nothing about you.

Get it

One command. It downloads the newest release, checks its published checksum and signature, and says what it will do before it writes anything.

sh -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/peterwalker78/slipstream-desktop/main/install.sh)"

Then log out and choose Slipstream on the login screen. Tap Super for your apps, Super+Enter for a terminal, Super and the arrows to move around, Super+Tab for bullet time, and Super+/ for every key and the tour.

Add -- --try to open it in a window on the desktop you're using now, installing nothing. Read install.sh before you run it. You'll need an x86_64 machine with Debian 13's libraries or newer, and graphics with working Mesa drivers. Everything about installing.

Two companion apps

They share the same aim, and the installer asks about each one first.

Glimmerwood

A quiet, lightweight browser. Its wisp, a small flame in the toolbar, brightens when you read, learn or make something, and grows sleepy in an endless feed. It never blocks or nags.

Westering

For the end of the day: a few quiet minutes under tonight's real sky, drawn in the same fine dots as the wallpapers, to set down what's on your mind. It has no network access at all.
