the-mundane-insane-loop.opus is "The Mundane Insane" by REKLAW, from the soundtrack album The
Buzz: the 1.96 seconds that lead into its drop, then from the drop to where the track comes back
round to it, twice over without a break, about six minutes.
the-mundane-insane-hint.opus is the drop's first 0.4 seconds, faded out.
