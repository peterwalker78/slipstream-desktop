the-mundane-insane-loop.opus is thirty-two bars of "The Mundane Insane" by REKLAW, from the
soundtrack album The Buzz, the loud section and the breakdown after it, played twice over
without a break. the-mundane-insane-hint.opus
is its first 0.4 seconds, faded out.
